Calculation details

Method: indirect efficiency = 100 − (dry flue gas + H₂ in fuel + moisture in fuel + moisture in air + CO + radiation & convection + unburnt in fly ash + unburnt in bottom ash) losses, as in IS 8753 and BEE Guide Book 2, Chapter 2, on GCV basis. CO₂ is computed from the fuel's burnt carbon; CH₄ and N₂O use IPCC 2006 default factors (kg/TJ, NCV basis) with AR5 GWPs (28, 265); electricity uses the CEA grid emission factor. CO₂ from biomass is biogenic and reported separately. Results are an energy audit estimate, not a certified measurement.
